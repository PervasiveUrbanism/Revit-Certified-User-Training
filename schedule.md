# Training Schedule

The programme is structured as short one-hour sessions over lunch. It assumes learners have little to moderate Revit experience and aims to prepare them for the Autodesk Certified User Revit for Architectural Design certification.

The schedule below runs across June and July. This may overlap with summer holiday arrangements, so attendance will be monitored as the course progresses. If too many people are missing from several sessions, the schedule will be adjusted.
