# ACU Objective

This course is designed to bring learners up to the level of the Autodesk Certified User in Revit for Architectural Design. The ACU is the entry-level Autodesk Revit certification. The next step after this is the Autodesk Certified Professional in Revit for Architectural Design, which assumes a much deeper level of project experience.

The ACU level is best understood as the minimum practical knowledge needed to operate Revit safely and efficiently in a project environment. It does not make someone an expert, but it should give them enough confidence to understand the interface, model basic architectural elements, create views, annotate drawings, produce sheets, and recognise where to look when they need help.

Some ACU preparation courses are delivered intensively over two or three days. This programme stretches the same foundation over approximately two months so that learners can practise between sessions, connect the topics to live office work, and absorb the ideas at a more realistic pace.

At the end of the course, learners should be in a position to attempt an online certification test if they want formal evidence of their knowledge. In practice, the real learning continues on live projects. Revit takes time and repetition: the aim is to remember the core concepts from the lessons, know what to look up, and become comfortable using Autodesk Help and project standards when new situations appear.
